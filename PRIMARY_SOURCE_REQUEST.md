Only lawfully accessible copies should be supplied. A publisher PDF, author manuscript, institutional repository scan, or clear scan of the relevant pages is acceptable if its identity and pagination can be established.

**M. Månsson et al. (1977), *Enthalpies of vaporization of some 1-substituted n-alkanes*.**  
DOI: `10.1016/0021-9614(77)90202-6`

Please provide the complete paper if possible. At minimum, the next audit needs:

- title/authors/journal page;
- apparatus and calorimetric method;
- sample identity and purity;
- every numeric result table and its headings;
- uncertainty/error/repeatability discussion;

Potential payoff: 13 candidate rows and up to 12 additional training molecules.

## Acceptance checklist

A row can be considered for the strict pool only if the full source permits all of the following to be answered:

- exact compound identity and sample state;
- directly reported numeric enthalpy and unit;
- measurement or reference temperature;
- measurement method and source-table locator;
- uncertainty/precision or an explicit statement that none is supplied;
- correction lineage, including any pressure-derived term;
- independence from frozen evaluation targets; and
- duplicate/overlap relationship to already approved labels.

Missing information is recorded as missing; it is never imputed to obtain approval.
